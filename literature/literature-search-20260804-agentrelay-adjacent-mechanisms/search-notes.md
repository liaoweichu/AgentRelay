## Exclusion Policy

- MDPI and other policy-excluded venues were not used.
- Search snippets, blogs, and secondary summaries were discovery aids only and are not evidence links.
- Papers whose only connection was generic “edge AI” or generic “agent safety” were excluded.
- Recent preprints are labeled as preprints; no acceptance status is inferred.

## Unknowns And Novelty Risk

- Cordon, Causal Past Logic, SAGA, and other 2026 work are recent and may change archival status or implementation availability.
- Retained-paper abstracts establish claim boundaries, but a frozen novelty statement still requires complete full-text inspection and citation chaining.
- The proposed joint gap may still be attacked as a composition of Arca, RAMP, Cordon/Atomix, and Hera. The empirical defense must show a non-additive interaction rather than a bundle of independent improvements.
- “Evidence-carrying” is deliberately used instead of “proof-carrying.” Formal proof language is prohibited unless the runtime gains a defined logic, proof object, checker, and soundness statement.

## Handoff To Idea Optimization

- Reframe the transferred object as an obligation-closed semantic continuation.
- Build a dependency graph over goal constraints, evidence, world state, plan obligations, raw trace references, and effect records.
- Select the smallest dependency-closed cut needed by the declared next-step obligations.
- Validate closure at the target and fetch named predecessors when closure is violated.
- Jointly choose executor, payload, and commit mode using measured cost and calibrated risk; abstain to stay/full replay when uncertain.
- Couple migration legality to the effect frontier instead of claiming a new generic transaction runtime.
