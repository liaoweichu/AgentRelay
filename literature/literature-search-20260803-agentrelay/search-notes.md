## Excluded Sources

- Policy-excluded or low-quality venues: not used in the final table.
- Secondary summaries and search snippets: used only for discovery, never as the final evidence link.
- Papers with only weak topical overlap: omitted from the retained set but named above where they help define the search boundary.

## Unknowns

- Several 2026 papers are preprints or workshop papers; their final archival status may change.
- Some OpenReview pages intermittently require a browser challenge; metadata was cross-checked through accessible paper records or PDFs when possible.
- Full reproduction packages for Hera and some very recent routing papers were not confirmed during this search.
- A claim of joint novelty remains conditional on full-text inspection of the retained Risk papers and successful empirical isolation of the proposed mechanisms.

## Handoff Notes

- For idea optimization: pivot from step-level routing to reliable, stateful handoff transactions. Treat routing as one component rather than the paper's sole novelty.
- For experiment design: require switch-cost measurements, state-fidelity tests, repair traffic, side-effect/retry correctness, end-to-end task success, and Pareto analysis.
- For writing: position against four clusters rather than a flat router bibliography; distinguish archival results from preprints/workshops.
- For review: likely reviewer attack is "systems integration of known components"; counter only with a joint formulation, routing-reversal evidence, and strong component interactions.
